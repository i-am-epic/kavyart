The Artist

Kavya

Every stroke of color, every intricate pattern, every flowing silhouette begins with a vision — to celebrate the rich tapestry of Indian fashion heritage through the art of illustration.

From the ornate bridal lehengas of Rajasthan to contemporary fusion silhouettes, each design is hand-rendered with meticulous attention to embroidery detail, fabric draping, and cultural authenticity.

This portfolio represents a journey through India's diverse textile traditions, reimagined through a contemporary artistic lens.
